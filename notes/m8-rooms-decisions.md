# M8 phase 1: rooms decisions

Ambiguities the rooms agent resolved (Glasswater Fen G1–G8, the Drowned Foundry F1–F10, K10's storm door, the Fen rows of `data/progression.ts`). Same columns as `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-26 | How do the Fen rooms join `data/progression.ts` without changing the M4 campaign? | A second table `fenRooms` (g1…g8, f1…f10) beside `regionRooms`, and `worldRooms` = both. `guaranteedRunes`, `roomChests` and validation use `worldRooms`; the campaign bot, the M4 region tests and the room shots' M4 list keep `regionRooms` | `campaignOrder = Object.keys(regionRooms)` drives the campaign bot and the M4 tests walk that list; adding the Fen there would send the M4 bots into a level-18 region. The map groups rooms by id prefix already, so `fen` and `foundry` are their own map groups with no code change |
| 2026-10-26 | Where does K10's storm door go? | In the arena's north-west corner: the exit `^` at (1, 0), the door `D` at (1, 1), the pylon and switch in the wall beside it at (2, 0) and (3, 0). Rows are unchanged | An exit tile has to be on the edge row, so the door can't stay in the wall row at (15, 0). A row added above the room would shift every K10 key in old saves (its Loom Stone `loom_stone@11,17`); a door in the top middle of the grass changed the kiter bot's seeded Warden fight (1 death, botFights.test.ts). The corner keeps the fight as it was and saves load unchanged. The switch's pylon sits next to the exit so the exit tile's ground resolves to stone (a tile with two object neighbours would resolve to `object`) |
| 2026-10-26 | Enemies and bosses that aren't merged yet | Every Fen room spawns region-1 stand-ins on the markers CONTENT §13 needs (`fenRooms.test.ts` `FEN_SPAWNS` holds the real lines, `it.skip` until the enemies and bosses work lands): `pike_shoal` → `bog_nest`, `crab_cast` / `heron_wade` / `mire_herd` / `toad_chorus` → `caller_guard`, `sparkfly_cloud` → `wisp_flight`, `foundry_crew` → `tar_crew`, `bellshell` / `slagshell` → `tar_hulk`, `bellwether` → 2 `tar_hulk`, `glasswater_coil` → 3 `bog_lurker` | Validation must stay green and rooms must load (an unknown kind throws at spawn). Stand-ins keep the rooms playable end to end; F5 and F10 are then not boss rooms, so they pay their runes when the stand-ins die and F10's Loom Stone is awake from the start, until the real bosses arrive |
| 2026-10-26 | `bellwether` and `glasswater_coil` in `regionBossKinds` now? | Yes | Nothing spawns those kinds yet, so it changes nothing; when the bosses arrive their rooms become boss rooms with no further edit (cleared on the boss's death, F10's Loom Stone asleep until then, rewards at the boss) |
| 2026-10-26 | G3: how is the third switch "cut off" robustly? | The third sealed switch sits in a dry sump whose only non-wall neighbour is the ice-block plug at the bottom of the sluice pool; it has no metal network of its own | Any metal touching it would be within Lob range of the floor (Lob arcs over walls), so a Lob[Storm] over the sump wall would skip the Fire step. With the plug as its only neighbour, only meltwater can carry charge to it |
| 2026-10-26 | G4: how does wading cost "about 6 pulses" when a pulse's water charge reaches only 600 cells and 40 steps? | The wade route is the heron canal, a walled 1-tile shallow sluice round the west of the lake (~240 cells), with the two pylons in niches of its wall and two rusted sluice walkways in it that pass the charge on (water hop 1, walkway hop 2, water and lake hop 3). Every pulse charges the whole canal; wading it takes ~14 s and 6 pulses in the test. The straight crossing (~64 cells) is deep glass to freeze | Wading 60 cells of open shallows takes 3 s, so at most 2 pulses: the design's numbers need a long route. A canal in the open lake would share the 600-cell budget with the lake. The north walkway also charges the north end of the lake, so the Frost path still crosses charged water on ice |
| 2026-10-26 | F8: the sealed switch "on a metal plinth" 28 cells from the pool | The switch stands on a stone dais | A metal plinth would be charged by any Storm hit on it, so the Floodline would be optional. On stone, only borrowed water touching the switch carries charge to it |
| 2026-10-26 | G7 and F8 need two doors with different conditions; the format links all switches of a room to every switch door | A second door character `d` with its own `@link` lines; a brazier, switch, plate or vane counts for the door whose links watch its kind, and when two doors watch the same kind (F8), for the nearer door (objectBuild.ts `linkFor`, objectLinks.ts). `@note` uses an object's own file character, so `@note d` works | The smallest change that keeps every existing room identical (one linked door: everything counts for it, as before) and needs no new header syntax. CONTENT §6.1 and §6.2 document it |
| 2026-10-26 | The Windward Cache's Pattern Seed and F1's chest have no legend character | `$` is a loot chest: it pays `data/progression.ts` `roomChests` once (RoomFlags.taken): `pattern_seed` (hero.patternSeeds + 1, event `patternSeedGained`, a HUD toast), `part` (a random non-boss Fuse part at the room's part tier) and `currency` (n rolls). Everything goes straight to the hero, like guaranteed runes. Validation checks every `$` room has contents and vice versa | Key chests only hold keys. Floor drops at a solid chest's center could land inside it |
| 2026-10-26 | F4 "can't be solved without Fire": Storm's rule 14 spark (15% per dry flammable cell, oil included) lights the oil channel too | Kept: the test's attempt has neither Fire nor Storm. A Lob[Storm] on the channel will usually light it | That's DESIGN rule 14 working as written; a channel Storm can't light would be a different material. The puzzle still teaches "fire runs along oil" |
| 2026-10-26 | F2's 3 s window | Two single casts from one spot do it (Lob[Storm] in the sump wakes two, Bolt[Storm] at the north pump the third) | CONTENT names weaves that help (Split, Chain, Echo, Sigil, Saturate) without requiring one; a 3 s window for two quick casts reads as a timing puzzle, not a build check. Feel call for the playtest |
