# M8 phase 1: render decisions

Ambiguities the render agent resolved (Fen palette, fog, wind, vents, borrowed terrain, fireflies). Same columns as `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-26 | How does the renderer know a room is in the Fen? | By room id: `fen/…` and `foundry/…`; render test rooms `test/fen_…` and `test/foundry_…` look the same. Rifts keep region 1's look, Fen chunks included | CONTENT §13 says "by region", and the id carries the region. A rift stitches chunks of several regions into one room; a per-chunk look can come later if playtests ask |
| 2026-10-26 | What changes in the Fen and the Foundry beyond grass, metal and water? | Fen: cool flagstones, lichen-slate walls, rusted teal pylons. Foundry: the Fen's look with darker sooted metal, soot on stone and sooted brick walls. Region 1 is untouched (same shader paths) | One coherent palette per region (DESIGN §15); "keep region 1 unchanged" |
| 2026-10-26 | Where does fog draw? | Above the floor, walls, objects and loot; below terrain fire and hazard edges, telegraphs, enemies, the player, smoke and enemy shots. Fire and charge thin it and tint it (warm, violet); lit braziers part it; the player's lantern clears a circle | DESIGN §14: fog never hides threats; telegraphs, hazard edges, enemy outlines, charge, fire and lit braziers draw above it. Enemies are drawn whole above it, the simplest way to keep their outlines |
| 2026-10-26 | Lantern radius and fog opacity: data or palette? | `tuning.atmosphere` (lantern radius 12, softness 3.5, fog opacity 0.93, brazier clear 9, firefly density 1.2), in the F2 panel. Visual only; the sim never reads them | They are feel calls the designer should tune (CLAUDE.md) |
| 2026-10-26 | How do lit vents look? | The packer feeds `vent_lit` cells to the fire pass as fire 190, so they get the same flames, glow and world-owned hazard edge as burning ground; the floor shader draws a glowing grate under them, and a heat shimmer and sparks rise. Doused vents are the bare grate with steam wisps | A lit vent is solid flame that ignites whoever touches it (rule 30): it should read exactly as fire, with the pulsing hostile edge (DESIGN §14) |
| 2026-10-26 | How does borrowed terrain flicker? | In its last 1.5 s it blinks to the ground it returns to (its `under`), at 3, 5 then 8 Hz, showing more of the under ground as it nears the end. Borrowed ice shows no thaw look (it returns to its ground, not to water) | "Look like their normal selves but flicker": the blink shows what the cell is about to become, which is the read the player needs. Stepped rates keep the blink's phase steady |
| 2026-10-26 | How are gusts drawn? | `#E8F4F0` dashes along the gust's direction with a dark under-stroke, plus a faint pale wash over the gust's cells, all fading with `gustTimer` (full for its first 1.4 s). Room wind: sparse faint dashes, slower | DESIGN §6.6; the under-stroke keeps a pale streak readable on the Fen's pale stone and water |
| 2026-10-26 | Fireflies: where, and how deterministic? | Render-only, positions from a hash of the room id: over water and reeds, 40% of them kept to metal under fog when the room has any, drawn to orbit lit braziers within 14 cells. Only in the Fen and Foundry | DESIGN §14 ("gather around metal in fog, a gentle hint where pylons are"); the hash keeps shots stable |
| 2026-10-26 | Pylon warning timing | The Live Wires warning now uses `pulseSeconds` (rifts and `@pulse`) and `tuning.world.pulseGlowSeconds` (0.8 s), the sim's own `pylonGlow` timing, instead of a render-side 1 s | One telegraph for one mechanic: what the player sees matches the sim |
| 2026-10-26 | Timed switches | A powered switch (`S` or `Q`) of a `timer:N:switch:S` group shows a Storm-yellow ring that empties as it holds, flashing in its last second | The player needs to read how long each switch stays powered to finish the group |
