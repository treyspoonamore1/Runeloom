# pt8boss decisions

Playtest 8 boss notes (the Glasswater Coil's head, boss state readability). Rows: date, question, decision, reason.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-27 | Where did the Coil's head go ("detached from the body and moved off the screen")? | Render only. `CoilRig.head` pulled the head back through each Lunge windup with `lift.position.x -= …` every frame, and `EnemyView` reset only `lift.position.y`, so the x offset summed over every windup frame (about 18 art units × the facing's x, 36 cells for a Lunge straight east or west) and never came back. Fixed in `EnemyView`: the lift's base position is set on both axes every frame (and in the death topple), so rig offsets are relative to it. | A headless probe of the sim head (boss bot with and without Tide, F10 and the test arena, seeds 1–3, plus scripted Frost and stone-standing fights) logged every tick: the sim head never entered a wall or left the grid, and never moved more than 0.73 cells a tick (the Lunge's 44 cells/s). The render test reproduces 35 cells of drift from one westward Lunge before the fix. |
| 2026-10-27 | Where does the boss state line get its numbers? | One read-only module, `src/app/bossStatus.ts`, picks the state from the sim's own flags (`BossState.fen.coil` for the Coil; `special.openLeft` and `special.dashSpeed` for the Bellwether) and fills the percentage from the live multipliers (`tuning.bosses.coilSubmergedTaken` / `coilDiveTaken`, `tuning.swim.strandedTaken`, the bell chemistry's `shelledTaken` / `openTaken`). The boss bar and the pop label both read it. | A retune can't make the bar lie, and the two readouts never disagree. Copy lives in `data/ui.ts` `bossStateCopy`. |
| 2026-10-27 | What does each state line say? | Submerged · N% less damage; Surfaced · full damage; Exposed · full damage, can't dive; Stranded · 50% more damage; Iced · can't move or attack; Overloaded · stunned; Shelled · 80% less damage; Rung open · 25% more damage; Rolling · can't be rung. | "Full damage" on Surfaced and Exposed contrasts with Submerged; Iced and Stranded name what each does, so Frost (Iced) and Tide (Stranded) read apart. Rolling says the one thing that changes (it can't be rung); it still takes the shelled 80% less. |
| 2026-10-27 | Rung while rolling: which wins? | Rung open, because `bellTakenMult` checks only `openLeft`. | The line must match the damage the sim applies. |
| 2026-10-27 | Should the bar itself change? | Guarded states (Submerged, Shelled, Rolling) dim and desaturate the life fill; the line is colored by tone (blue guarded, gold open, ice-white helpless). | A second cue that hits do less, without touching damage numbers or hints (the pt8hits agent's scope). |
| 2026-10-27 | Pops: event-driven or state-watching? | `render/bossPops.ts` watches each Fen boss's state each frame (`BossPopTracker`, no Pixi, unit-tested) and pops STRANDED, ICED or OVERLOADED on a change into that state (any state with a pop in `bossStateCopy`). A boss seen for the first time doesn't pop; a new pop replaces the boss's old one. | Works the same after loads, room changes and replays, and the boss bar and pop share one definition. In the tide boss-bot fights the pop count equals the sim's strand and ring counts (3 STRANDED). |
| 2026-10-27 | A RUNG pop over the Bellwether? | No. The orchestrator reported that the pt8hits branch already shows a gold "RUNG!" label, flash and ding over any enemy the moment it is rung (shared event code), the Bellwether included; a second pop would double it. The Bellwether keeps its boss bar line ("Rung open · 25% more damage"). | Avoids two labels for one event; `bossStateCopy.rung.pop` is null. |
| 2026-10-27 | Where do the feel numbers live? | `tuning.bossReadout` (`data/tuningBossReadout.ts`), so they show in the F2 panel: pop seconds, size, rise and lift; Coil submerged opacity, tint and water veil, ripple rate, surfaced brightness, splash droplets, foam rate. `FEN_BOSS_FEEL.submergedAlpha` and `rippleRate` moved there. Rigs read it through a new `BeastCtx.tuning`. | CLAUDE.md: feel calls go in tuning and the panel. |
| 2026-10-27 | How does Submerged read as under the water? | The Coil's body and head get a film of water color over them (`CoilBody.veil`, a head veil sprite), on top of the existing dimming and deep tint, with ripples along it; only the eye stays lit. Surfaced and Exposed are brightened glass; breaking the surface throws a bigger foam splash and ring; while Surfaced in deep water foam rings form around the head; Exposed drips. | The old Submerged was a translucent dark tint that still showed the full inked outline, so in the arena it read as "on the water", like Surfaced. |
