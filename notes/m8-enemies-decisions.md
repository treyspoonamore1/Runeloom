# M8 phase 1: Fen enemies, decisions to merge into docs/DECISIONS.md

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | How do swimmers follow water as it appears? | A bounded breadth-first search over water cells from the swimmer (8 neighbors, no corner cutting, `tuning.swim.planCells` = 3,000 cells), re-planned every 12 ticks: it heads for the reachable water cell nearest its goal, 3 steps along the route (`actors/swim.ts`). Bog Lurkers use it too, so they now wait at the shore nearest the player by route, not by straight line | Tide lure paths bend; a straight-line drift stalls at the first corner. A per-swimmer search costs little (a few swimmers per room) and needs no shared field. The Coil reuses `swimToward` |
| 2026-10-25 | "Is this cell water" for rule 31 | One helper, `isWaterCell` (`actors/locomotion.ts`): the material's `water` is shallow or deep. Borrowed water counts by its material and stops counting when it returns | The brief: one small helper, so borrowed water works when the chemistry agent's merge lands |
| 2026-10-25 | Rule 31 "within 16 cells" | 16 steps over open ground (8 neighbors, anything shots cross: pits too), found by the same search; the crawl moves over anything shots cross | Stranded swimmers can sit in a drained pit (the Coil); walls still block. Steps match how it has to crawl |
| 2026-10-25 | Ice under a swimmer: trapped or Stranded? | Its water freezing under it traps it (M4 rule, rule 31 "not ice"). Landing on ice from a leap strands it (CONTENT Glasspike). Trapped on ice that then returns to dry ground (borrowed ice, rule 27) strands it | Both rows of the docs hold; the transition covers Tide then Frost |
| 2026-10-25 | Glasspike: "within 16 cells of its water" | It swims to the water nearest the player, so the trigger is the player within about 16 cells of the pike (leap range 13 cells edge to edge); the landing circle is radius 3; the bite resolves on touchdown over that circle | It waits at that shore, so the two distances are the same in play. Radius matches how other "N-cell circle" rows are stored |
| 2026-10-25 | Copperback loops | Charge pulsed by a conductor body never charges a conductor body; other enemy charge (a Spark Heron's strike) does | Two crabs in one pool would keep each other Charged for ever |
| 2026-10-25 | Spark Heron's spit target | A targeted 0.8 s marker at the player's position, only when the player's cell isn't water; until Tide lands the puddle is `placeTempWater` on hydromancerGrounds (stone, dirt, grass, ash, mud) | "Spits puddles under a dry player"; its water can then be struck through |
| 2026-10-25 | Slagshell "Cracked for 6 s" vs "heats again after 6 s" | Cracked is held while Wet and lasts 6 s after it dries (so a Tide hit, Wet 3 s, cracks it for 9 s). The quench places real steam (rule 4's steam around its cell) | Reads both phrases as one rule; the steam puff is real chemistry (it Blinds) |
| 2026-10-25 | Sparkfly: how does "one Storm hit pops a cloud over water in a chain" work with 75% Storm resistance and enemy-owned pops? | A Sparkfly hovers low: charge reaching it from the water or metal under it pops it outright if it is the player's, the world's, or another Sparkfly's pop; other enemy charge (Copperback pulses, Heron strikes) passes it by. Its pop then electrifies 8 cells of its water as enemy charge, which pops its neighbours. Chain pops are credited to whoever killed the first Sparkfly while that one is still in the enemy list (else the world). Direct Storm hits still meet the 75% resistance | With hit damage, neither the player's charge nor a 6-Storm pop can kill one through 75%, and enemy charge can't hurt enemies at all, so no chain could happen. Designer check: is an outright pop too strong? |
| 2026-10-25 | Sparkfly lure duration | Lured for 3 s, then it looks again; a light still burning keeps it. It still zaps a player who comes within reach while circling | "Sparkflies swarm to every lit brazier" (G7) |
| 2026-10-25 | Mirebull's charge length and stuns | The run is the distance to the player plus 6 cells, at most 36; cells on ice cost 1/1.5 (so up to 54 on ice). A run planned into a wall or object stuns it 1.5 s; one stopped by deep water or a pit doesn't. Mud anywhere on the run (its start included) bogs it. Dashes ignore ice easing (they run at dash speed) and the slide after one starts from no more than top speed | The Warden's overshoot pattern; "hitting a wall" read literally |
| 2026-10-25 | Bellows Toad's gas | Lit by any hit carrying Fire during the croak's windup, whoever deals it (Ignite ticks are not hits); the puff is a world-owned fire burst scaled by area level only (a world hazard); the 15% life loss is credited to the hitter | "Hit with Fire while inflating"; world hazards don't take region or tier scaling |
| 2026-10-25 | Bellshell: does it open when it bites? | No, it stays shelled while biting; only a ring opens it. The ringing charge's own hit lands on the open shell. Open 5 s, then the 6 s cooldown starts | "Teaches conduction as a key": a melee window would undercut it |
| 2026-10-25 | regionDifficulty and bosses | Fen and Foundry monsters get the tier 8 multipliers where a rift tier's apply (life at spawn, their hits on the player in combat/hits.ts); bosses keep their absolute CONTENT numbers, and world hazards don't scale | CONTENT §5: boss numbers are absolute; DESIGN §10.4: world hazards don't scale with the tier |
| 2026-10-25 | Rule-only Fuse parts' tier element | Pike Scale's tiers II and III add increased Storm damage (the Fen's element). Bell Bronze applies to every Physical hit of the player's on a Shocked enemy (Tide and Gale are the obvious ones). Toad Bellows' 30% more area is a radius × sqrt(1.3) (`playerGustScale`, for the Gale gust code) | Every no-number part needs an element for its tiers; the parenthesis in CONTENT names examples |
| 2026-10-25 | Sparkfly pop reach | An optional `waterReach` on grid/charge.ts `ChargeSource` (2 lines): a chain's own water reach and falloff distance. The Coil's Eye and Body pulses need the same | Rule 11's reach is per owner today; the pop reaches 8 cells |
