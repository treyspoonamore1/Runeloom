# M8 phase 1: chemistry decisions

Ambiguities the chemistry agent resolved (grid foundations, Tide, wind). Same columns as `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | How do fog cells remember that they were cleared? | Fog is `air = fog` with `airTimer` 0. A cleared fog cell has `air = none` and `airTimer` counting the seconds until it rolls back; any cell whose smoke, steam or clear timer ends gets `restingAir` (fog where `fogMask` is set, else none). Walls inside a `@fog` rectangle get the mask bit but never fog | Reuses the one air timer and the active set, so fog costs nothing while quiet and needs no new per-cell field. Smoke over fog then returns to fog when it ends ("replace it while they last") |
| 2026-10-25 | Is a lit furnace vent a burning grid cell? | No. It is a material (`vent_lit`, solid, not shot-blocking) that ignites entities touching it (rule 30). It doesn't spread fire to its neighbors, light oil, or clear fog | DESIGN §6.2 gives it no spread chance and rule 30 lists only entity ignition. A vent that lit the grass around it would make every vent room burn down on load |
| 2026-10-25 | Do pre-M8 rooms get the new header keys? | No: `fog`, `wind` and `pulse` exist on the parsed header only when the file has those lines | "Room files from before M8 load unchanged": their parsed headers are identical objects, so nothing that compares or serializes headers moves |
| 2026-10-25 | Do pre-M8 states keep their hashes? | Yes. `wet` (now u16) hashes as its low bytes, plus its high bytes only when some cell exceeds 255 ticks; `borrowed`, the gust fields, `wind`, `fogMask` and the gust list are hashed only when any is set | Every scenario hashes identically before and after the M8 grid fields, which is a strong check that the new code is neutral where it isn't used |
| 2026-10-25 | Do `@pulse` pylons and Live Wires glow first? | Both send `pylonGlow` `tuning.world.pulseGlowSeconds` (0.8 s) before each pulse; Live Wires' pulse timing is unchanged | CONTENT §6.2 says `@pulse` works "as Live Wires does" and adds the glow; one telegraph for one mechanic. Events aren't hashed, so rifts replay as before |
| 2026-10-25 | How is a direction quantized to the 8 winds? | Sector test with tan 22.5° (no `atan2`) | Bit-identical in every engine; the sim never depends on trig rounding |
| 2026-10-25 | "Borrowed water's timer goes back to 10 s": what if it has more left (a Lingering Tide)? | It is set to the new Tide's time only if that is longer (the timer never shrinks). A doused vent hit by Tide again also gets its full time back | "Goes back" reads as a refresh; shortening a Lingering flood by casting more Tide would punish the player for the obvious move |
| 2026-10-25 | Rule 32 Wet and the same hit's own ailments | Wet is applied after the hit lands (after its Ignite, Chill, Shock). So `[Fire, Tide]` or `[Tide, Fire]` never leaves an Ignite, and the hit that wets a target doesn't arc (rule 19) on itself; the next Storm hit does | Simplest order that can't Wet a target the hit didn't reach; "it puts out Ignite" holds for the hit's own fire too |
| 2026-10-25 | A push or ice block over borrowed terrain | The cell keeps `borrowed`, its timer and its origin in `under`; the borrowed ground the block hides goes in a new u8 field `cover` (code + 1). Moved off early, the cell gets that ground back with its time left; if the time runs out under the block, the block simply rests on `under` | `under` already meant two things for a covered borrowed cell (the block's floor and the borrowed origin). Without a second slot a block would freeze Tide water or Glaze ice for good, or wipe the ice path as it slid along it |
| 2026-10-25 | What "touching" a lit vent means (rule 30) | The body's edge is within `tide.ventTouch` (0.5 cells) of any lit vent cell. Floating and fixed kinds are above the ground and aren't ignited. Pyre Walker's immunity to burning ground applies | Same reading as rule 16's "burning ground"; a vent is fire on the ground |
| 2026-10-25 | How a relit vent "pushes anyone standing on it off" | Right after each grid tick (step.ts, before blasts), any ground body overlapping a lit vent moves to the nearest safe spot at least ventTouch + 0.05 cells clear of solid cells, with no fall damage, and sends `pushedOff`. The terrain phase does the same as a fallback instead of rule 25's fall | The movement collision runs first in a step and would leave the body pressed against the flame, where rule 30 ignites it; the spec says the relight never ignites |
| 2026-10-25 | Rule 28 details | A block is washed when any cell of its tile is in the Tide footprint and it is at rest. The direction is the spell's direction along its stronger axis (Bolt, Beam, Strike, Swath), else away from the footprint's center (Nova, Lob, Orb, and non-spell Tide such as the Lab). Once per block per spell instance (`washId` on the block); Tide without a spell id washes every time | Blocks move on the 4-axis tile grid like pushes; a block mid-travel already occupies its tile and shouldn't be shoved twice |
| 2026-10-25 | Enemy puddles' ground | `placeBorrowedWater(state, x, y, r, seconds)` covers the Hydromancer grounds (stone, dirt, grass, ash, mud) | "On dry ground"; the same list Hydromancer already uses, so the two kinds of borrowed water behave alike |
| 2026-10-25 | Which braziers stay lit under Tide | A brazier stays lit if a door with its `@link` is open; any other lit brazier goes out | "Unless its group already opened its door" (DECISIONS); unlinked braziers have no door to keep |
| 2026-10-25 | Is a Nova's (or an Orb's) gust one gust or one per band? | One per spell instance: every write with the same spell id joins one gust record (each cell keeps its own end tick), so a Nova painting ~12 bands or an Orb's 12 trail circles count once against `wind.maxGusts` | Otherwise a single Nova would hit the 8-gust cap by itself and end its own first bands |
| 2026-10-25 | Which cells does a gust write? | Every cell of its footprint except shot-blocking ones (walls, objects); no line of sight. A radial gust skips the cell exactly at its center (no direction) | Gusts are a terrain footprint like any other; walls can't hold air, so they never drift smoke or spin vanes through themselves |
| 2026-10-25 | Do terrain-duration stats lengthen gusts? | Yes, like Lingering: gust time = wind.gustSeconds × Lingering × terrain duration | Gale's gust is its terrain effect; every other terrain effect takes those stats |
| 2026-10-25 | When does a wind vane count 0.5 s? | Wind must cover a cell touching its tile 0.5 s in a row; a gap resets the count. The vanes of a door that opened start spun on re-entry, like switches | "Spins while wind covers it for 0.5 s, then stays spun", read as continuous; latching like a switch keeps solved rooms solved |
| 2026-10-25 | How is the rift's room wind applied? | `riftRules(state).wind` (of the Gale: east) is written into `grid.wind` when a rift room without its own `@wind` is entered (world/transition.ts), so the renderer and every system read one place | One source of truth for "the room wind"; the rift chunks stay plain text |
| 2026-10-25 | Does the Gale suffix need a tier gate before the gate agent adds one? | Yes: `fromTier` on rift modifiers (Gale 9), filtered in `rollSigilMods` | Without it the new suffix would roll on tier 1 sigils the moment it exists. The filter leaves the RNG draws of lower tiers unchanged |
| 2026-10-25 | Fire spread under wind, measured | Room wind or a gust east, 8 seeds × a 16-row grass line fire, 2 s: downwind 1.65×, upwind 0.38× the windless count (factors ×2 and ×0.25; the across neighbors and the burn-out keep the ratios inside them) | The acceptance bounds (≥ 1.6, ≤ 0.4) hold with the DESIGN factors; no tuning change needed |
| 2026-10-25 | Lab stations that need objects (rule 28, vanes) | The lab room builds only its push blocks, ice blocks and wind vanes outside adventure mode (`LAB_OBJECT_KINDS`), steps only those, and `runStation` puts them back on their tiles | The lab runs in sandbox mode, where rooms have no objects; the Loom Stone and the rest stay out so the M3 bays play as before |
| 2026-10-25 | Lab station order with M8 rules | The 13 M8 stations fill two new bay rows (4 and 5) in rule order, after the M3 rows, which keep their bays; three bays are spare. The lab test checks rule order within each block | Re-sorting all 45 would move every M3 bay and its expectations for no gain |
| 2026-10-25 | Rule 32 in the Lab | A lab action `wet` gives the Tide rune's Wet (3 s) to the enemies in the bay, with a Tide step on the ground at the same time | Lab scripts paint terrain only; rule 32 is a hit effect |
| 2026-10-25 | Reference weave 17 "about twice the cells in 2 s" | Measured with the DESIGN numbers (cone 12 cells, 2 s, ×2 downwind), 6 seeds on a grass field: cells burnt past the Strike's arc are 1.67× without Gale after 1 s and 1.25× after 2 s; inside the cone both versions burn out every cell by 1.5 s. The test pins 1.5–2.5× at 1 s and > 1.15× at 2 s. **Flagged for the designer**: "twice in 2 s" needs a longer gust, a longer cone or a larger downwind multiplier (all in tuning.wind) | The design numbers are the spec; changing them is a feel call for the designer, so the test states what they give and the report asks |
| 2026-10-25 | Reference weave 18's Tar Hulk | The lob's Tide burst Wets the Hulk (rule 32), its Ignite goes out and with it the fuse ('hulkFuseOut'); the grass under the burst stays wet 10 s and won't relight | "A burning Tar Hulk's fuse goes out" (CONTENT §2) through the same Wet as rule 15 |
| 2026-10-25 | What the wind bench times and loads | `grid:wind` (tools/lib/gridWindBench.ts): grid:typical's workload plus `@wind e`, a new 12-cell Gale-size gust every 5 grid ticks (7.8 live on average, the cap is 8) and smoke topped up to at least 30% of the active cells. Each sample is stepGrid alone. With wind, the fire's own smoke drifts into new cells and covers ~67% of the active cells, so the top-up never runs: the bench is harsher than DESIGN §12 asks | Times the grid tick's budget exactly; a harsher smoke share leaves margin rather than hiding cost |
| 2026-10-25 | Drift cost | Drift steps per direction are computed once per grid tick, the target's shot blocking is read from `room.shotSolid`, and the source's fog check is skipped where the fog byte is 0 | The first version's p99 (~2.2 ms) came from the every-8th-tick room-wind drift of ~10k smoke cells; now ~1.6 ms p99 alone (typical: ~1.2–1.4 ms) |
