# M7 Shatter trigger: decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | What counts as "you shatter an enemy" for the Shatter trigger? | A death of a Frozen enemy credited to the player (`killerId === PLAYER_ID`), queued in `killEnemy` right beside `noteKill`. That covers the player's hits and the player's Ignite (DoT kills credit whoever applied the Ignite), and a lit Tar Hulk blast credited to the player's Ignite. Monster-credited kills (an enemy's hit or Ignite), world kills (no source) and console kills (`killall`) don't count. | Same crediting rules as Harvest (On Kill trigger), so the two triggers never disagree about whose kill it was. Freeze state is read at death (`shattered` in the 'death' event), the same flag rule 20's frost burst uses. |
| 2026-10-25 | Does a shatter also count as a kill? | Yes: a player shatter queues both a 'kill' (Harvest) and a 'shatter' event; a weave with either trigger fires. | A shatter is a kind of kill; CONTENT doesn't say Harvest should skip shatters. |
| 2026-10-25 | Raising `RUNTIME_SHIPS` from M5: what else unlocks? | Raised to `shipsThrough('M7')`. No rune ships M6 and Shatter is the only M7 rune; Tide and Gale (M8) stay refused ("Tide arrives in M8"). No Shatter-specific gate needed. | `grep "ships: 'M" data/runes` shows only shatter (M7) and tide/gale (M8) above M5. The sandbox pouch and the app's `ALLOWED_SHIPS` follow `RUNTIME_SHIPS`, so Shatter appears in the sandbox palette (unlimited) and needs a rune item in adventure mode, like every rune. |
| 2026-10-25 | Does the DPS readout force Shatter events like On Dodge / On Hurt / On Crit? | No. It gets a note instead: "Shatter can't fire against dummies, so its damage isn't included." Existing notes and numbers are unchanged. | Readout dummies are immortal, so nothing dies or shatters; Harvest and On Kill aren't forced for the same reason. Forcing would invent a kill rate the readout has no basis for. |
| 2026-10-25 | Where does a Shatter weave start and aim, and what's its source? | From the shattered enemy's position, aimed away from the player (player → enemy; the player's aim if they coincide), with the dead enemy as source, through the existing `fireFromEntry` path that Harvest uses. | `origin: 'shattered_enemy'` and `aim: 'away_from_caster'` match Harvest's origin/aim handling exactly; no new runtime path needed. |
| 2026-10-25 | ICD and mana with several shatters on one tick? | One fire per 0.3 s per slot (18 ticks); extra shatters inside the ICD are dropped, not queued for later. Mana is the weave's cost × 0.6 (compiled from the rune's `manaMult`), paid on fire; short on mana it skips and starts no ICD. | Same as every other automatic event trigger. |
