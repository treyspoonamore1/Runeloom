# M7.5 inventory pass: decision rows

For `docs/DECISIONS.md` (the merge copies them there). Date, question, decision, reason.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-25 | How is "is this an upgrade?" scored? | Score = sum of weight × value over the rows of the character sheet (`statRows`), swapped into the item's slot with `heroModsOf`. Weights live in `data/ui.ts` `inventoryUi.upgrade`: per unit (1 life 0.1, 1 mana 0.05, 1 mana/s 0.5, 1 Strike damage 0.4, 1 Capacity 6, 1 Dodge charge 4) and per percentage point (damage increases 0.25, branch damage 0.15, crit 0.6, cast speed 0.4, reduced mana cost 0.3, each resistance 0.3, move speed 0.3, projectile damage taken 0.4, dodge recharge 0.1, ailment, Ignite, chill, Freeze 0.1, terrain duration and fire spread 0.08). A green arrow needs a gain of at least 0.5 | The sheet is the one list of stats the player sees, so the arrow can't count something the sheet hides. Resistances are capped in the sheet (`min(cap, raw)`), so overcapped resistance counts 0 for free. The weights say how many points of each stat the designer would trade for 10 life; they are a feel call, so they sit in data, not code |
| 2026-10-25 | When does a bag item get the red down arrow? | Only when it is strictly worse: at least one sheet stat falls and none rises, and the score gain is below the 0.5 threshold. A trade (more life, less resistance) shows no arrow when its score gain is small, and a green one when the gain is at least 0.5 | The brief called the red arrow optional. Strictly worse is the one case where no weighting is needed to say "don't bother" |
| 2026-10-25 | Which slot does a charm compare against, and equip into? | An empty charm slot first, else the one where the swap scores best (the weaker charm). The same function (`bestSlot`, upgrade.ts) drives the arrow, the compare tooltip, the sheet preview and click or right-click equip | One rule everywhere, so the tooltip never says "replaces X" and then replaces Y |
| 2026-10-25 | Does a plain left click still equip? | Yes. Right-click also equips. Shift-click salvages, Ctrl or Cmd-click sends to the bench, K keeps, Delete asks to salvage (always with a dialog), arrows move, Enter equips | Left click equipping is the existing habit and drag and drop stays. Delete keeps its dialog because it is a key you can hit by accident, where shift-click is deliberate |
| 2026-10-25 | Shift-click salvage confirmation | Normal and Magic go at once with a toast "Salvaged <name>: +N Thread Scrap" (`note`, the menu's own toast; there is no undo). Rare and Unique open the existing salvage dialog. A kept item or a spindle with fused parts says why it stays | As asked. The salvage dialog already existed |
| 2026-10-25 | What does bulk salvage never take? | An equipped item, a unique (rarity or `unique` key), an item with a fused part, an item with `locked`. Presets: All Normal, All Normal and Magic, everything the loot filter hides. Parts are not touched. The dialog lists each preset with its count and Scrap and the button repeats both | Matches the brief. `protectedReason` (salvageSel.ts) is the one place that decides. "Salvage hidden" (M5) is now the third preset; its bar opens the dialog on it. The M5 version already skipped worn and fused items; it now also skips unique and kept ones |
| 2026-10-25 | Lock ("keep") on the sim side | `Item.locked?: boolean`, set only when true (a lock-off deletes the field). HeroEdit `{ kind: 'lock'; uid; locked }` (equip.ts `setLocked`) works on any owned item, worn or not, in adventure mode. `salvage` refuses a locked item with "Unlock it first". `hashItem` adds `locked` only when set; `saveParse` accepts a boolean (true keeps, false is read as not locked) and rejects other types | Optional field, no save version bump: older saves load, and saves and replays without locks keep their hashes. Normalizing false to absent means one hash per state |
| 2026-10-25 | Is the Salvage Loom (Hearth) affected by Keep? | No. It salvages pickups as they arrive; nothing it takes can be locked yet | A new item is never locked |
| 2026-10-25 | Bag sort orders | Slot (spindle, cloak, hood, boots, charm; rarer then higher item level first within a kind), Rarity (unique first), Item level (highest first), Newest (highest uid first). Ties fall through the other keys, then uid | Stable orders, so tiles don't swap places between hovers. Newest is the uid order because uids only grow |
| 2026-10-25 | Where are the bag's sort and filters remembered? | `localStorage` key `runeloom.inventory.v1` as `{ sort, slot, rarity, upgradesOnly }`, validated on load, every access in try/catch (`bagPrefs.ts`). Defaults: slot order, no filters | Per viewer and presentation only, like `settings.ts`. Not in the save |
| 2026-10-25 | How does the compare tooltip avoid covering the sheet? | A bag item's tooltip is two cards (the item, what you wear in its slot) plus a table "If equipped: stat, now → after, change", placed below or above the tile and kept inside the bag panel (`placeWithin`, frame.tsx), so the sheet on the right stays readable and shows each stat's change in green or red | The sheet preview and the tooltip both answer "what does this change", so they must be visible together. The old side placement hid the sheet |
| 2026-10-25 | Affix tier text | `src/ui/items/affixText.ts`: "+26 to maximum life" with "T3: 20–29" in small text after it (tier 1 is the best; the range is the tier's min–max from data/affixes.ts). A tier missing from the data shows "T3" alone. Fixture items (ids that aren't in data/affixes.ts) show plain lines | Shared with the crafting bench |
| 2026-10-25 | Fuse parts: one tile per part or stacks? | Parts of the same kind and tier stack with a count; click fuses one, shift-click salvages one. Runes already stacked | Parts of one kind are interchangeable. A stack is much easier to scan than five identical tiles |
| 2026-10-25 | Minimum text size | 11 px in the inventory (12 px for body text): `--t-xs`, `--t-s`, `--t-m` in invStyle.ts are `max(11px, n × --u)`. The tab bar, headings, dialogs and tooltips under `.inv-root` use them too. Other screens are untouched | At 390 wide `--u` is 4.4 px, so the old 1.4 u captions were 6 px. The frame is shared, so the floor is applied only below `.inv-root` |
| 2026-10-25 | Where do the new inventory screenshots run? | On a real game (not `?fixture=1`): level 12 hero, bag rolled with `rollGear` and `rollSpindle` from the seed, uniques, kept items, parts, currency (`tools/lib/invShots.ts`). The old `inventory` and `fuse` shots stay on the fixtures | The fixture bases (wool_cloak, linen_hood) aren't real item bases, so ItemArt and the tier text would show placeholders for them |
| 2026-10-25 | Crafting currency in the sheet | The sheet lists owned crafting currency with CurrencyArt and the count; a click opens the bench. Details are in the tooltip | The wallet there showed only Scrap, Seeds and Shards, so stacks of Knots and Shuttles were out of sight on this screen |
