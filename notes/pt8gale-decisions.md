# Playtest 8: Lob[Gale] visual decisions

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-27 | How to shoot one weave at fixed moments after its cast? | New `--set spells` in `npm run shots` (tools/lib/spellShots.ts): a sandbox game in test/fen_render, the weave in slot 1, cast once, shot at ticks 15, 30, 34, 38, 45 and 90 after the cast (or `--ticks N`) | No lab station or scenario casts one spell and stops at chosen moments; sandbox mode needs no rune items |
| 2026-10-27 | Is the gust a Lob[Gale] writes wrong in the sim? | No change to the sim. It writes the 5-cell burst circle, each cell blowing away from the landing point, quantized to 8 directions: exactly DESIGN §6.6's row "Nova, Lob, Orb: the footprint, away from its center", and tests/sim/terrain/galeGusts.test.ts already checks it | The weird look came from how render drew that field, not from the field |
| 2026-10-27 | Why did the Lob[Gale] gust look like a grey snowflake? | The atmosphere shader read the gust direction bilinearly and drew straight lanes in world space per direction. In a small radial gust, opposite directions cancel at the center (grey speckle noise where the direction spins per pixel), the 8 sectors' seams bend lanes into jagged spokes, and the rim curls. Now the direction comes from the pixel's own cell (texelFetch), and radial gusts are drawn in polar lanes from their center, cleared within about 1.5 cells of it | Nova[Gale] had the same artifacts, hidden under the player; Lob shows the center in open ground |
| 2026-10-27 | Where does the render get a radial gust's center? The sim's Gust keeps only cells and a key (the spell id) | Render-only registry (src/render/terrain/galeBursts.ts): centers from 'lobLand' events with Gale and from live Gale Novas, matched to grid.gusts by key; reach = the farthest written cell + 0.75; at most 8 drawn as bursts. Orb trails (many small circles) keep the per-cell streaks | Keeps the sim and its hash unchanged; a Saturate, Wide or Toad Bellows burst gets its real reach from its cells |
| 2026-10-27 | Burst streak dash, speed and lane count | tuning.atmosphere.galeBurstDash 4 cells, galeBurstSpeed 14 cells/s, galeBurstLanes 18 (F2 panel, render only) | Feel values; the cone streaks' 8-cell dash at 22 cells/s gave a 5-cell burst only one dash per ray |
