# pt8hits decisions (playtest 8: reduced hits, hints, Bellshell readability, long notes)

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-27 | Which reductions mark a hit as "reduced"? | Only target states: Shelled (any `chemistry.bell` enemy whose shell isn't rung open: Bellshell 90%, Old Bellwether 80%) and the Glasswater Coil Submerged (75% / 90%). Elemental resistances never count. | No simple uniform rule for resistances: the Coil resists 75% Storm everywhere, and greying every resisted hit would drown the signal the playtest asked for (a state you can change). |
| 2026-10-27 | How does the sim say why? | `'hit'` events from `applyHit` on an enemy carry an optional `reduced: 'shelled' \| 'submerged'` (`actors/chemistry.ts reducedBy`), set only when present so other hit events are unchanged. `dealDirectDamage` (falls, gas life loss) never carries it: those ignore multipliers. | Events are output only: `state.events` is not hashed (hashState.ts), so the state hash and replays are unaffected. |
| 2026-10-27 | Where does "already shown" live for the hints? | UI side, once per page session: one `ReducedHitHints` watcher in the adventure HUD (src/ui/adventure/reducedHints.ts). Not saved in hero flags. | Simplest; the sim, saves, the state hash and replays never see it. A reload can show a hint once more, which is harmless for a teaching line. |
| 2026-10-27 | Where do the hint thresholds go? | `data/tuning.ts hints` (reducedHits 5, windowSeconds 8 of sim time), as asked, so they show in the F2 tuning panel. The toast's on-screen seconds (7) are presentation and live in `data/ui.ts uiTuning.hintToastSeconds`; the texts in `data/ui.ts reducedHints`. | tuning.ts is cloned into GameState and stored in replays; the sim never reads `hints`, and cloneTuning fills it in for old replays, so nothing changes there. |
| 2026-10-27 | How do hints show? | A toast (kind 'hint') with its own icon (a brass shell with a small shield; deep water), a title line and a wrapping second line, kept 7 s. Only the player's own hits count. | The toast column is the existing small-note UI; the center banner is for room names and boss cards. |
| 2026-10-27 | How does a reduced hit look and sound? | Render: a small (14 vs 17) dull grey number with a steel shield mark on its left, no pop, a shorter rise; a small grey clank glint and a few metal sparks instead of the element sparks (render/damageNumbers.ts, fxView.ts reducedHit). Sound: the usual hit sound at 0.35 (`audioTuning.reducedHitGain`) under a new dull `shellClank` preset (data/sfxHits.ts). | Reads as "that barely went in" next to a normal white number, without new text to learn. |
| 2026-10-27 | Where do the new presets live? | A new data/sfxHits.ts (shellClank, bellDing), merged into sfxM4 and routed by src/audio/fenBossCues.ts. | sfxFenBosses' own test holds its presets to ≥ 400 ms gaps and ≤ 0.35 gain (boss telegraphs); a clank on every reduced hit needs a short gap. |
| 2026-10-27 | What did the Bellshell's rung state lack? | Before: at the ring moment nothing marked the ring itself (only the charge hit, drowned in the electrified lake); open, the shell lifted 1.4 cells with a cream tint and pale cream waves that barely show on pale water; the Rung icon was a dark disc like the others. Now: the ring moment gets a gold body flash (0.3 s), a gold glow, gold and ink rings, a glint and sparks, a gold "RUNG!" label, a light shake and the `bellDing` sound; open, the shell lifts 2.1 cells, takes a pulsing gold tint, its waves are gold; the Rung icon is a gold disc. | Playtest: "I couldn't figure out what I was supposed to do with the turtles/bells". |
| 2026-10-27 | Does the Old Bellwether get the ring moment too? | Yes for the event-driven parts (flash, burst, RUNG! label, ding, reduced numbers, hint), sized by its radius; its rig (bellwetherRig.ts) and the boss bar are untouched (pt8boss owns them). The Rung icon change applies to both, since they share it. | Same rule, same teaching; the rig and the bar are another agent's. |
| 2026-10-27 | How do long @note texts fit? | A note banner gets the class `rl-note`: it wraps (`white-space: normal`, `text-wrap: balance`) at min(88u, 100vw − 32px), at 3.4u instead of the room title's 6u. Room-name and boss banners are unchanged. | F7's 85-character note now takes two lines at 1280×720 and at 960×540. |
