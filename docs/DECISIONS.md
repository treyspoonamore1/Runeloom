# Decisions

Ambiguities resolved during development, and designer decisions that differ from the docs. Newest last.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-18 | Which TypeScript version? | 5.9.3, not 7.x or 6.x | typescript-eslint 8.71 supports TypeScript below 6.1; 5.9 is the most widely supported. |
| 2026-10-18 | Which Preact version? | 10.29.8, not 11.0 | Preact 11 shipped this month; 10.x is what the Vite preset and signals are proven on. |
| 2026-10-18 | Which Playwright version? | 1.56.1 | Matches the Chromium build preinstalled in the cloud dev environment. Locally, run `npx playwright install chromium` once. |
| 2026-10-18 | Where do player and enemy bodies live in src/sim? | `src/sim/actors/` | The CLAUDE.md layout had no home for movement and bodies. |
| 2026-10-18 | How is "no DOM in the sim" enforced? | `tsconfig.sim.json` typechecks `src/sim` and `data/` with no DOM or Node types, and ESLint bans `Math.random`, `Date`, `performance` and render/UI imports there | Mistakes fail `npm run check` instead of relying on review. |
| 2026-10-18 | Is the sim deterministic across all browsers? | Guaranteed within V8 (Node and Chromium). The sim avoids transcendental math where it can; replays from other engines may diverge | IEEE basic ops are identical everywhere, but `Math.sin` and friends are not specified bit-exactly. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does tuning reach the sim? | Each GameState owns a copy of the tuning values. The F2 panel edits the live copy. Replays store the tuning they were recorded with | Keeps the sim pure (no global mutable config) and replays exact. |
| 2026-10-18 | Units | Positions and distances in cells, time in seconds, speeds in cells per second. 1 tile = 4×4 cells, 1 cell = 8 px at 720p | DESIGN §4 and CONTENT §6. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does a dodge start, and are presses during a dodge buffered? | Starts moving on the press tick. Presses during a dodge are ignored, not buffered | Simplest; revisit after playtest. |
| 2026-10-18 | What happens when a dodge ends? | It ends at run speed along the dodge (or wall-slid) direction, then normal acceleration or deceleration takes over. With no input this adds about 0.46 cells | No dead stop after a dodge (DESIGN §4 feel). |
| 2026-10-18 | How do dodge charges recharge? | One at a time, each a full `dodgeRecharge` after it was spent, leftover time carried to the next | Matches "1.4 s recharge per charge" in DESIGN §4. |
| 2026-10-18 | How fast is sliding when pushing diagonally into a wall? | At the along-wall share of the push (about 0.71 × speed) | Physically consistent; revisit if it feels sticky. |
| 2026-10-18 | How is collision resolved? | Substeps of at most 0.4 cells; push out of the deepest overlapping solid cell first, up to 6 times; fall back to the last clear position | Deepest-first prevents snagging on cell seams; substeps prevent tunneling at dodge speed. |
| 2026-10-18 | Where do debug UI panels sit? | Stats and F1 menu top-left, F2 tuning top-right, console full-width at the bottom (26% of height) | Keeps the center of the screen clear. |
| 2026-10-18 | How do scenario inputs reach the browser in tests and screenshots? | Node computes the input frames, then the page steps its live session with them | Scenario inputs depend on state, so precomputing in Node keeps both sides identical. |
| 2026-10-18 | Values CONTENT §5 leaves open for M1 enemies | Radii: Tinderling 1, Shieldbearer 2, Ember Archer 1.4, dummy 1.5. Nip: circle r 1.5, 0.3 s recover, 1 s cooldown. Shield bash: 90° cone r 5.5, knockback 3, 0.6 s recover, 2 s cooldown. Arrow: 45 cells/s, radius 0.6, 1 s life. Every attack is active for 0.1 s. Shieldbearer takes half knockback; dummies take none | Starting values for playtest; all in data/enemies.ts. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does a cast release, and how is mana paid? | The cast starts on press; the spell releases at 25% of the cast time (tunable), and mana is paid then. Holding repeats. A dodge or death cancels an unreleased cast. Movement is allowed while casting | Responsive feel with a short wind-up; DESIGN §4 allows casting while moving. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does hitstop work? | Sim-side: the longest pending hitstop wins (not the sum); frozen ticks hold positions; presses during hitstop are dropped | Deterministic and replayable. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does knockback decay? | Velocity shrinks linearly by DT/τ per tick (τ = 0.08 s), with impulse = distance/τ, so the travel equals the CONTENT distance without `exp` | Exact distances, no transcendental math. |
| 2026-10-18 | Hits during i-frames | No damage, no knockback, an 'evaded' event; enemy arrows fly on. Respawn grants 1 s of i-frames | Dodging through arrows should feel clean. |
| 2026-10-18 | Do rooms spawn their @spawn enemies? | Yes, in createGame (opt out with `spawnRoomEnemies: false`) | Rooms are self-describing. |
| 2026-10-18 | Game modes and cheats | GameState has `mode` ('sandbox' or 'arena') and `cheats.god`. Both are hashed and stored in replays | They change the simulation, so replays must know them. |
| 2026-10-18 | Default scene | `arena` (endless waves) from M1 on; `?scene=room` opens the M0 test yard | The arena is the playable demo. |
| 2026-10-18 | Mana economy too tight for M1 (bot finding) | Provisional, pending designer playtest: mana regen 3% → 8% of max per second; Tinderling life 18 → 14 so one Strike kills one at area levels 1–2. Tinderlings keep 75% fire resistance, so Fire Bolt is still the wrong tool against them | With 3% regen a bolt-only clear was impossible (about 28 bolts in 90 s), and Strike (16) left fodder at 2 life. |
| 2026-10-18 | M1 AI choices | Arena mode skips room @spawn enemies; arena enemies always hunt the player. Enemies aggro when hurt and alert idle packmates within 12 cells. `cheats.noAI` (hashed) freezes AI for tests. Flow field on the tile grid; only its goal tile and build tick are hashed. AI uses its own `aiRng` stream. Shield bash triggers only within ±37° of facing (the cone is ±45°) | See src/sim/ai. |
| 2026-10-18 | Arena waves | Wave 1: 6 Tinderlings. Wave 2: 8 Tinderlings + 2 Ember Archers. Wave 3: 7 + 3 archers + 2 Shieldbearers. Then 6 + 2n enemies with archers rising toward 35% and Shieldbearers toward 25%, capped at 80. Area level +1 every 3 waves. First wave 2 s after start, 3 s intermissions | Data in data/waves.ts and tuning.arena. |
| 2026-10-18 | HUD and audio choices | Wave start/cleared banners and sounds come from arena state changes (the sim has no wave events). Auto-pause on window blur. Esc opens the pause menu unless the dev console is open. zzfx is loaded on the first click because importing it opens an AudioContext | Browsers block audio before a user gesture. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does the screen shake? | Provisional: Strike kills (3 px), crits (1.2 px) and player hurt (1.6 px), plus Shatter and explosions later. DESIGN §4 said Shatter and explosions only. A settings toggle turns it off | M1 has no shatters or explosions, so hits needed some weight. Revisit after playtest. |
| 2026-10-18 | How do the player's own effects dim when busy? | Busy = enemies + projectiles/4 + particles/40; between 40 and 110 the player's own effects fade toward the effect-density setting. Telegraphs, enemy shots and hurt/death effects never dim | DESIGN §14 readability. |
| 2026-10-18 | Enemy rendering | Enemies are sprites from a texture atlas baked from vector shapes at startup | 300 vector enemies per frame was too slow; the look is unchanged. |
| 2026-10-18 | Debug stats panel | On by default in dev builds, off in release builds; F3 toggles it in both | The demo build shouldn't open with a debug panel. |
| 2026-10-18 | M1 playtest (designer) | Movement, dodge, hitting and readability feel good; every enemy has a clear role. The provisional M1 values are confirmed as-is: mana regen 8%/s, Tinderling life 14, screen shake on Strike kills, crits and player hurt. No tuning changes | Designer playtest notes. The "Fire Bolt when mana is high, Strike when low" rhythm emerged and was liked. |
| 2026-10-18 | Should "melee is cheaper" be a rule? (designer question) | No hard rule. New guideline "Closer pays more" (DESIGN §2.1): close range pays back in damage, mana or defense; ranged stays viable through investment. First test in M2: Strike restores 2 mana and Nova 1 mana per enemy hit within 8 cells of the player, at most 6 per spell instance | Designer approved. Distance, not melee versus magic, is the axis; a refund makes close range generate the resource ranged spends, instead of nerfing ranged. |
| 2026-10-18 | M2 sandbox loadout | Unlimited copies of every M2 rune and a choice of five spindles, in every build including the published demo, until drops arrive in M4 | One copy of each rune can't fill four weave slots, and Twig's 8 Capacity can't build half the reference weaves. |
| 2026-10-18 | Ailment details (M2) | Ailment strength uses hit damage after resistance but before Shock (Shock never feeds itself). Equal-strength ignite or shock doesn't refresh. Ailments apply only if the target survives the hit; evaded hits apply none. Freezing consumes all chill; no chill builds while frozen; chill caps at 100. Boss freeze immunity starts at thaw. A DoT kill on a frozen enemy shatters without hitstop. God mode: ailments apply but ignite deals no damage to the player. Chill doesn't slow the dodge. Death and respawn clear player ailments. One `actionSpeed` helper (0 frozen, else 1 − slow) scales movement, AI, attack phases and cast progress | Simplest consistent reading of DESIGN §6.4. |
| 2026-10-18 | Spell language choices (M2) | Milestone gating is checked with unknown runes ("Fork arrives in M5"). Structure checks run across the whole tree in order: empty slot, missing element, Sigil without Emit, depth. A branch under an element-less Strike has nothing to inherit and fails. Elements can't repeat in a spell. Wide/Focus scale area, so radii grow by the square root; Saturate doubles the ground footprint the same way. Inherited and infused elements add no mana. Heavy's slower cast is cast time ×1.15. Automatic triggers report a cast time of 0. On Hurt aims at the cursor. Seeker has no range limit. Rotations use Taylor series, no trig | Simplest consistent reading of DESIGN §5 and CONTENT §1. |
| 2026-10-18 | Weave editing contract | UI code uses only src/app/loadout.ts (getLoadout, previewWeave, setWeave, setSpindle, slotTimers) and runReadout (src/sim/weave/readout.ts). Invalid weaves never replace a working one; a spindle switch keeps weaves that no longer fit, but they don't fire until they fit | Lets the editor and the runtime be built in parallel; the player never loses a working slot to a typo. |
| 2026-10-18 | Ailment visuals (M2) | Status icons are a fixed 13 px on screen at any zoom. Animated ailment parts (flames, crystals, arcs) are pooled particles drawn above enemy bodies; only the ice block is part of the body sprite. Free ailment particles dim with the player's own effects; icons and the player's own ailments never dim. Particle spawns are capped at 450/s. Shatter shakes 2.4 px | Readability with 300 enemies; performance. Render cost with 300 ailing enemies measured 6–8 ms in software rendering on a loaded machine (baseline without ailments 4–4.6 ms there); re-measure on real hardware. |
| 2026-10-18 | Loom editing rules (M2) | Tab closes the Loom after mouse use but moves focus when navigating by keyboard; Esc closes a popup, then cancels a picked rune, then the Loom. New elements go after the last element, modifiers at the end. Removing a trigger or form leaves a dashed spot; an empty draft empties the slot. Rune swaps that would remove an element the form needs aren't offered; form swaps that break a spell are shown blocked with the reason. Typed notation applies on Enter, blur or close, never mid-typing. The previous DPS number stays visible, dimmed, while the next computes. Weaves and spindle persist in localStorage (`runeloom.loadout.v1`) | Clear editing with no surprises; a half-typed weave never replaces a working one. |
| 2026-10-18 | Spell runtime choices (M2) | Triggers run after enemies each tick: Cast, then Pulse, then queued events (dodge, kill, crit, hurt — hashed queue). Pulse fires on its first tick, then every period; a skipped pulse restarts it. Branches fire in weave order. Over 2,000 live instances, the oldest end as "evicted" without On Expire. Chain jumps are instant, after the first hit, and ignore shields. Echo's −20% applies to the repeat only, not its branches. On Expire's source is the enemy the spell stopped on. DoT kills count for Harvest but not On Kill. Nothing fires while frozen or dead. An Orb follows the player only when it started at the player. Lob and Sigil with direction-only aims land at full range. Mana refunds count before the max-mana clamp. Session resets keep the weaves | Simplest reading of DESIGN §5.3 that keeps runaway triggers bounded. |
| 2026-10-18 | DPS readout for close-range spells | Strike, Nova, Swath and Orb roots can't reach dummies 20 cells away, so the readout moves the dummies to 0.6 × the spell's reach and notes it | Otherwise close-range weaves would read 0 DPS. |
| 2026-10-18 | Form visuals and sounds (M2) | Spell sounds play on spell spawn, not cast release; branches at 55% volume, Echo repeats at 50%. Element-less melee forms tint gold; elemental Strikes get a 60% element wash. Beam and Swath use normal blending (additive bleaches on light ground). Tide and Gale use the physical look until M8. Form effects dim with the player's effects | Echoes, branches and automatic triggers all need their own sound and look. |
| 2026-10-18 | Known tech debt after M2 | data/spells.ts (M1 spell table) is still imported by player.ts, runtime/loadout.ts and the HUD icons; remove when M4 brings rune items. The 300-enemy + 2,000-bolt stress bench has thin p99 headroom (2.34–2.40 ms vs 2.7 ms on a quiet machine). Render cost with 2,000 live bolts measured ~8 ms in software rendering | Tracked so M3 (grid) doesn't push them over budget unnoticed. |
| 2026-10-18 | M2 playtest (designer) | The Loom is clear; no runes feel useless or mandatory. The designer built a Strike weave specifically to generate mana for more complex weaves, which is the intended "Closer pays more" loop. No tuning changes. Watch item: if a Strike "battery" becomes mandatory in every build, lower `combat.manaOnHit.capPerInstance` from 6 to 4 (break-even instead of profit) | Designer playtest notes. |
| 2026-10-18 | Grid contract (M3) | Materials and their numbers live in data/materials.ts (codes never renumbered); tile → ground mapping in data/tileGround.ts (markers take the most common neighbor ground, ties → stone). The grid is `state.grid` (src/sim/grid/grid.ts), hashed; timers count grid ticks. Everything that changes terrain goes through `applyElement(state, { element, shape, owner, sourceId, hitDamage, durationMult })` (src/sim/grid/paint.ts). Puzzle objects and doors are an inert solid `object` material until M4 | Lets the rules engine, the grid renderer and content be built in parallel. |
| 2026-10-18 | Chemistry Lab (M3) | 63×41-tile lab: 8×4 bays of 6×6 tiles opening south onto walkways, 32 stations sorted by rule (plus barrel and three combo showcases). Runner restores a station's area from a pristine grid, clears entities in it, applies time-0 steps, spawns, then schedules the rest (hashed). R runs the station you stand at, Shift+R runs all; console `lab <rule|#index|name|nearest|all>` / `lab list`; these mark the session edited. Rule 20 uses a Shieldbearer frozen then killed by a lab action (dummies can't die). All lab applications use the player as source; the team comes from the step's owner | Every rule demonstrable in under ~3 s (bridges and ice bridges excepted, labeled). |
| 2026-10-18 | The Wilds arena | The `arena` scene now plays in test/wilds (river with a bridged deep pool, meadows, oil and barrels, a metal plaza, bramble hedges, pillars); the old flat arena is `arena_flat`. The bot also clears a 20-enemy wave there | A demo arena that shows off M3. |
| 2026-10-19 | Chemistry rules engine (M3) | Grid clock counts only non-hitstop ticks (grid ticks every 3rd). Spread and Storm ignition use a hashed `gridRng`. Active set: flags + sorted list; spread, dense smoke and explosions are collected during the visit and applied after, so order never matters. A barrel is its whole 4×4 tile and explodes from its center; the explosion spills oil, then lights it. Oil burning out ignites flammable ground under it. Light smoke covers the burning cell, dense smoke its 4-neighbors too; smoke/steam never change ownership or cover shot-blocking cells. Steam covers each hit water cell plus a 1-cell ring; melting ice makes no steam. Frost puts out fire and also freezes or dries the cell; frost on temporary ice restarts its timer. Rule 14 rolls once per hit cell; oil counts as flammable. One Storm hit is one chain: one event per body, up to 4 water↔metal hand-offs. A footprint covering no cell center affects the cell under its origin; lines are capsules. Lingering doesn't lengthen ice-bridge cracking or the barrel fuse. Explosions reach entities through `grid.blasts` (events are never read by the sim) | Deterministic, order-independent rules; bench 0.36 ms typical, 0.78 ms p99 at 256×256 with 21% active. |
| 2026-10-19 | Terrain renderer (M3) | Grid packed into RGBA8 data textures (codes, smooth fields with two copies for crossfading between grid ticks, hazard masks, a per-tile glow texture), uploaded only when `grid.version` changes and only the changed span. Three shader passes: floor (all materials, smooth material edges), fire (above walls and props), smoke/steam (above entities, below enemy shots and hit effects). Walls keep the baked M0 look and re-bake when wall/object cells change; crates, barricades and barrels are individual shapes that darken as they burn. Glow is baked into the shader instead of a pixi-filters bloom pass (departs from DESIGN §12, for performance). Enemy/world hazards pulse with the telegraph red-magenta at 1.6 Hz. Renderer forced to WebGL | Smooth 60 fps from 20 Hz grid ticks; readable hazards; no full-screen blur passes. |
| 2026-10-19 | Spells and entities on the grid (M3) | Each damaging form paints its elements in slot order as the player's team: Bolt a 2-cell circle where it ends, Nova each newly reached ring band (its Storm paints the whole disc once when the ring completes, so one Nova is one charge chain), Beam its line, Lob its burst, Strike its arc, Swath its strip, Orb a circle under itself every 0.25 s, Sigil nothing. Storm charge carries the spell's Storm damage. Frost no longer freezes water under steam, so [Fire, Frost] ends as steam over water (DESIGN §5.3). Terrain-fire Ignite applies fire resistance and is credited to the player for player-owned fire, to the world otherwise; player i-frames dodge terrain fire and charge. Charge hits once per charge event with falloff. Arcs (rule 19) reach only entities the hitting team can hurt, nearest first, one generation. Falls (rule 25) cost 10% max life and return to the last safe cell; ice bridges and wood over water or pits don't count as safe. Blind caps sight at 4 cells without dropping a target. A Wet Tinderling goes out (2 s stun, ×1.5 damage taken) and leaves no death fire; missed arrows light only flammable cells; a shield burns only in fire that hurts its bearer. Enemies avoid hazards that hurt them (flow-field cost +8, local steering); Tinderlings steer straight through but share the flow field. The stress bench's bolts paint no terrain (fixed load); the Wilds bot bench carries the terrain load and times the grid tick separately | Simplest consistent reading of DESIGN §6.3. Wilds bot bench: entity step p99 0.36 ms, grid tick p99 0.29 ms. Stress bench p99 still needs a quiet-machine re-measure (2.39–3.84 ms on a loaded machine, merge base 2.32–3.10 ms in the same runs). |
| 2026-10-19 | Terrain effects and sounds (M3) | The hostile-hazard edge follows the outline of the whole hazard area and shows only on its hostile parts, so mixed ownership inside one pool draws no line. Grid events get one-shot effects (ignition flare, ash and steam puffs, frost bloom, ice chips and shatter, an electrify front racing across charged cells, explosions with a dark-backed shockwave that stays visible on sand). Explosions shake the screen (respecting the setting). A fire crackle bed follows the burning cells on screen and pans toward them; every terrain sound is quiet and rate-limited. Lab plaques wrap within their bay; numbers stay with their units; the lab hint sits bottom-left. Feel values live in `TERRAIN_FX`/`TERRAIN_ONESHOT` (src/render/terrain/terrainColors.ts) and `terrainAudio` (data/sfx.ts), not the F2 panel | Additive light washed out on the light floor; plaques and the hint overlapped. Terrain pack 0.77 ms per grid tick with the whole lab running. |
| 2026-10-19 | Creature effects and sounds (M3) | Entities on the grid get their own look, separate from ailments: Wet (cool tint, gloss, drips, droplet icon), Oiled (dark tint, hue-cycling sheen, oil-drop icon; Oiled + Ignited burns 1.4× bigger and redder with black smoke), falls (silhouette shrinks and spins into the gap, then pops up at the safe spot; the player's is bigger and never dims), charge hits (cyan-white zap, distinct from Shock's violet), arcs (0.15 s jagged bolts), doused Tinderlings (steam puff, ash-grey wash), shields that char as they burn and break into splinters, a closed-eye icon for Blind and a soft screen-edge haze when the player stands in smoke or steam, ice slide streaks. Enemy creature effects dim with the player's effects on busy screens; the player's never dim. Sounds in data/sfxCreature.ts, routed by src/audio/creatureCues.ts. Feel values in src/render/creaturePalette.ts | DESIGN §14 readability. Render cost with 300 wet/oiled/burning enemies about 2 ms above plain enemies in software rendering. Follow-ups: the oil-drop icon is hard to tell from the water drop at 1× zoom; Oiled + Ignited is hard to pick out inside a field that is already burning. |
| 2026-10-19 | How does the designer reach the Chemistry Lab in the demo? | A place picker in the pause menu (The Wilds, Chemistry Lab, Flat arena, Test yard) resets into that scene's room and mode and keeps the weaves. Lab plaques, the hint and the R keys follow the loaded room instead of the URL, so `room test/lab` also works. The start screen points to it | The published demo has no URL parameters, so `?scene=lab` was unreachable there. |
| 2026-10-19 | Lab screenshots (M3 acceptance) | `npm run shots -- --lab` captures each station as a storyboard: untouched, 0.4 s after each scripted step (first, middle and last when there are more than three), and the result at the time in its label (else 1.5 s after the last step), with the player watching from the walkway and god mode on. Reviewing them found three stations that didn't show their rule: the barrel blast never reached its grass (moved one tile closer), the Shatter Shieldbearer walked toward the watching player before freezing (now frozen at 0.05 s), and the rule 9 frost covered only part of the burning patch so the fire spread straight back (frost now covers the patch; its test now requires the whole patch to go out) | A single before/after pair missed short-lived effects (charge, arcs) and stations that build their own setup (ice bridges). |
| 2026-10-19 | Stress bench headroom (tech debt from M2) | Resolved without changing behavior: the 300-enemy + 2,000-bolt p99 was set by garbage-collection pauses (about 343 KB of garbage per tick, mostly floats boxed in module-level variables and array resets). Spatial-hash queries write into a reused typed array, separation and collision scratch values live in Float64Arrays, bolts skip enemy queries when no enemy bucket is near (`SpatialHash.anyNear`), and the DDA walks avoid the global `Infinity`. State hashes are identical on 1,732 checkpoints across every scenario. Remaining garbage is mostly new spell instances (`spawnSpell`) | Stress bench mean 1.10–1.21 → 0.84–0.88 ms, p99 2.41–2.76 → 1.83–1.99 ms (budget 2.7 ms). |
