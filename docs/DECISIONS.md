# Decisions

Ambiguities resolved during development, and designer decisions that differ from the docs. Newest last.

| Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-18 | Which TypeScript version? | 5.9.3, not 7.x or 6.x | typescript-eslint 8.71 supports TypeScript below 6.1; 5.9 is the most widely supported. |
| 2026-10-18 | Which Preact version? | 10.29.8, not 11.0 | Preact 11 shipped this month; 10.x is what the Vite preset and signals are proven on. |
| 2026-10-18 | Which Playwright version? | 1.56.1 | Matches the Chromium build preinstalled in the cloud dev environment. Locally, run `npx playwright install chromium` once. |
| 2026-10-18 | Where do player and enemy bodies live in src/sim? | `src/sim/actors/` | The CLAUDE.md layout had no home for movement and bodies. |
| 2026-10-18 | How is "no DOM in the sim" enforced? | `tsconfig.sim.json` typechecks `src/sim` and `data/` with no DOM or Node types, and ESLint bans `Math.random`, `Date`, `performance` and render/UI imports there | Mistakes fail `npm run check` instead of relying on review. |
| 2026-10-18 | Is the sim deterministic across all browsers? | Guaranteed within V8 (Node and Chromium). The sim avoids transcendental math where it can; replays from other engines may diverge | IEEE basic ops are identical everywhere, but `Math.sin` and friends are not specified bit-exactly. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does tuning reach the sim? | Each GameState owns a copy of the tuning values. The F2 panel edits the live copy. Replays store the tuning they were recorded with | Keeps the sim pure (no global mutable config) and replays exact. |
| 2026-10-18 | Units | Positions and distances in cells, time in seconds, speeds in cells per second. 1 tile = 4×4 cells, 1 cell = 8 px at 720p | DESIGN §4 and CONTENT §6. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does a dodge start, and are presses during a dodge buffered? | Starts moving on the press tick. Presses during a dodge are ignored, not buffered | Simplest; revisit after playtest. |
| 2026-10-18 | What happens when a dodge ends? | It ends at run speed along the dodge (or wall-slid) direction, then normal acceleration or deceleration takes over. With no input this adds about 0.46 cells | No dead stop after a dodge (DESIGN §4 feel). |
| 2026-10-18 | How do dodge charges recharge? | One at a time, each a full `dodgeRecharge` after it was spent, leftover time carried to the next | Matches "1.4 s recharge per charge" in DESIGN §4. |
| 2026-10-18 | How fast is sliding when pushing diagonally into a wall? | At the along-wall share of the push (about 0.71 × speed) | Physically consistent; revisit if it feels sticky. |
| 2026-10-18 | How is collision resolved? | Substeps of at most 0.4 cells; push out of the deepest overlapping solid cell first, up to 6 times; fall back to the last clear position | Deepest-first prevents snagging on cell seams; substeps prevent tunneling at dodge speed. |
| 2026-10-18 | Where do debug UI panels sit? | Stats and F1 menu top-left, F2 tuning top-right, console full-width at the bottom (26% of height) | Keeps the center of the screen clear. |
| 2026-10-18 | How do scenario inputs reach the browser in tests and screenshots? | Node computes the input frames, then the page steps its live session with them | Scenario inputs depend on state, so precomputing in Node keeps both sides identical. |
| 2026-10-18 | Values CONTENT §5 leaves open for M1 enemies | Radii: Tinderling 1, Shieldbearer 2, Ember Archer 1.4, dummy 1.5. Nip: circle r 1.5, 0.3 s recover, 1 s cooldown. Shield bash: 90° cone r 5.5, knockback 3, 0.6 s recover, 2 s cooldown. Arrow: 45 cells/s, radius 0.6, 1 s life. Every attack is active for 0.1 s. Shieldbearer takes half knockback; dummies take none | Starting values for playtest; all in data/enemies.ts. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does a cast release, and how is mana paid? | The cast starts on press; the spell releases at 25% of the cast time (tunable), and mana is paid then. Holding repeats. A dodge or death cancels an unreleased cast. Movement is allowed while casting | Responsive feel with a short wind-up; DESIGN §4 allows casting while moving. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does hitstop work? | Sim-side: the longest pending hitstop wins (not the sum); frozen ticks hold positions; presses during hitstop are dropped | Deterministic and replayable. |
| 2026-10-18 | How does knockback decay? | Velocity shrinks linearly by DT/τ per tick (τ = 0.08 s), with impulse = distance/τ, so the travel equals the CONTENT distance without `exp` | Exact distances, no transcendental math. |
| 2026-10-18 | Hits during i-frames | No damage, no knockback, an 'evaded' event; enemy arrows fly on. Respawn grants 1 s of i-frames | Dodging through arrows should feel clean. |
| 2026-10-18 | Do rooms spawn their @spawn enemies? | Yes, in createGame (opt out with `spawnRoomEnemies: false`) | Rooms are self-describing. |
| 2026-10-18 | Game modes and cheats | GameState has `mode` ('sandbox' or 'arena') and `cheats.god`. Both are hashed and stored in replays | They change the simulation, so replays must know them. |
| 2026-10-18 | Default scene | `arena` (endless waves) from M1 on; `?scene=room` opens the M0 test yard | The arena is the playable demo. |
| 2026-10-18 | Mana economy too tight for M1 (bot finding) | Provisional, pending designer playtest: mana regen 3% → 8% of max per second; Tinderling life 18 → 14 so one Strike kills one at area levels 1–2. Tinderlings keep 75% fire resistance, so Fire Bolt is still the wrong tool against them | With 3% regen a bolt-only clear was impossible (about 28 bolts in 90 s), and Strike (16) left fodder at 2 life. |
| 2026-10-18 | M1 AI choices | Arena mode skips room @spawn enemies; arena enemies always hunt the player. Enemies aggro when hurt and alert idle packmates within 12 cells. `cheats.noAI` (hashed) freezes AI for tests. Flow field on the tile grid; only its goal tile and build tick are hashed. AI uses its own `aiRng` stream. Shield bash triggers only within ±37° of facing (the cone is ±45°) | See src/sim/ai. |
| 2026-10-18 | Arena waves | Wave 1: 6 Tinderlings. Wave 2: 8 Tinderlings + 2 Ember Archers. Wave 3: 7 + 3 archers + 2 Shieldbearers. Then 6 + 2n enemies with archers rising toward 35% and Shieldbearers toward 25%, capped at 80. Area level +1 every 3 waves. First wave 2 s after start, 3 s intermissions | Data in data/waves.ts and tuning.arena. |
| 2026-10-18 | HUD and audio choices | Wave start/cleared banners and sounds come from arena state changes (the sim has no wave events). Auto-pause on window blur. Esc opens the pause menu unless the dev console is open. zzfx is loaded on the first click because importing it opens an AudioContext | Browsers block audio before a user gesture. |
| 2026-10-18 | When does the screen shake? | Provisional: Strike kills (3 px), crits (1.2 px) and player hurt (1.6 px), plus Shatter and explosions later. DESIGN §4 said Shatter and explosions only. A settings toggle turns it off | M1 has no shatters or explosions, so hits needed some weight. Revisit after playtest. |
| 2026-10-18 | How do the player's own effects dim when busy? | Busy = enemies + projectiles/4 + particles/40; between 40 and 110 the player's own effects fade toward the effect-density setting. Telegraphs, enemy shots and hurt/death effects never dim | DESIGN §14 readability. |
| 2026-10-18 | Enemy rendering | Enemies are sprites from a texture atlas baked from vector shapes at startup | 300 vector enemies per frame was too slow; the look is unchanged. |
| 2026-10-18 | Debug stats panel | On by default in dev builds, off in release builds; F3 toggles it in both | The demo build shouldn't open with a debug panel. |
| 2026-10-18 | M1 playtest (designer) | Movement, dodge, hitting and readability feel good; every enemy has a clear role. The provisional M1 values are confirmed as-is: mana regen 8%/s, Tinderling life 14, screen shake on Strike kills, crits and player hurt. No tuning changes | Designer playtest notes. The "Fire Bolt when mana is high, Strike when low" rhythm emerged and was liked. |
